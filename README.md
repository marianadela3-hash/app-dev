## My Favorite K-Drama: Crash Landing on You

**Genre:** Romance / Comedy / Drama
**Where to watch:** *Netflix*
**Network:** tvN

> A story about how love can happen in the most unexpected place.

### Why I Love It

1. The love story is sweet and emotional
2. The cast has great chemistry
3. It mixes comedy, romance, and drama perfectly
4. The soundtrack is beautiful

### Main Characters

- **Yoon Se-ri** – a rich South Korean businesswoman
- **Ri Jeong-hyeok** – a North Korean army captain
  - Calm, caring, and protective
- **Seo Dan** – a strong and stylish rival
- **Gu Seung-jun** – a charming and funny con man
